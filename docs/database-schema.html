BunTrack Database Schema

From prisma/schema.prisma. App tables not migrated yet

16 tables (10 new for the app, 6 for sign-in, already live on Neon) and 4 enums. Money is whole centavos (₱149.00 = 14900); stock quantities are the item's small unit (pc, g, ml).

How the tables connect

Read each line from the "one" side to the "many" side: one category has many products.

Relationships and delete rules

Cascade deletes the related rows too. Restrict refuses the delete while related rows exist, which protects history. The app archives products and items and deactivates people, so these are a safety net.

OneManyThroughOn deleteWhy
UserSessionuserIdCascadeA removed account's sign-ins go with it.
UserAccountuserIdCascadeThe password hash belongs to the person.
UserOrdercashierIdRestrictPast sales keep their cashier. Deactivate people instead.
UserStockMovementrecordedByIdRestrictThe stock history says who moved what.
UserExpenserecordedByIdRestrictSame, for expenses.
UserActivityEntryuserIdRestrictThe log can't lose its "who".
CategoryProductcategoryIdRestrictA category in use can't be deleted.
ProductRecipeLineproductIdCascadeA recipe means nothing without its product.
InventoryItemRecipeLineitemIdRestrictAn ingredient can't vanish from a recipe that uses it.
InventoryItemStockMovementitemIdRestrictThe ledger stays complete; archive the item instead.

ProductOrderLineproductIdRestrictSold products are archived, so receipts and reports stay correct.

Decisions to check

DecisionExampleWhy
Money is whole centavos₱149.00 → 14900Totals never pick up rounding errors.
Stock in small units2.5 kg onions → 2500, unit kgSmall deductions (15 g of lettuce) never drift.
Order lines are snapshotsunitPrice 16900Editing a product later never rewrites past revenue or profit.
Stock is a ledger+5000 Stock In, -15 saleEvery change is a row that's never edited; quantity is the running total.
Archive, don't deletearchivedAt setOrders, recipes and the ledger point at products and items.
"Who" links to the accountcashierId → UserA renamed family member shows their current name everywhere.
Order numbers from 1001#1001, #1002A database sequence, so two tills can't take the same number.
Some lists stay text"Ice Cream (Dan Eric)"Names with spaces can't be enum values; the app checks them.
